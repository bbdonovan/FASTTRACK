This instance is wired to a local Ollama model (Llama 3) for all /api/ask calls. You can still optionally store a Gemini API key here if you want to experiment with cloud models later.

Ask Johny 5 · Live Agentic Graph RAG

Questions here call /api/ask, which in turn hits backend.llm_analyze(). That function talks to a local Ollama LLM and orchestrates tools over company profiles, external metrics, recent news, geopolitical intel notes, and the graph neighborhood. The response includes (1) an answer and (2) a structured trace of every tool the agent used.

Why Johny 5 is Agentic (not just another RAG bot)

When you ask a question, Johny 5 doesn’t just search for a paragraph. It runs a mini-investigation across data sources and shows you the steps it took.

JOHNY 5 · AGENTIC MODE

Standard Graph RAG

  • Turns your question into a single vector.
  • Grabs a few similar documents.
  • Optionally follows simple graph links.
  • Sends one big context dump to an LLM.
  • Little visibility into how it answered.

Johny 5 · Agentic Graph RAG

  • Understands the question and detects tickers & themes.
  • Looks up company profiles and external metrics.
  • Pulls fresh news, filings-style items, and geopolitics intel.
  • Walks the knowledge graph neighborhood for related entities.
  • Then synthesizes everything into one analyst-style answer, plus a transparent tool-by-tool trace.

What Johny 5 just did for your question

  1. 1 Classified the question (tickers & themes).
  2. 2 Retrieved profiles & headline metrics.
  3. 3 Pulled recent news & filings items.
  4. 4 Checked sanctions / geopolitics intel.
  5. 5 Explored the graph neighborhood.
  6. 6 Wrote a concise, stitched-together answer.
