Why This Is Agentic (vs Standard Graph RAG)

When you click Ask Johny 5, the system does more than a simple vector search. It runs a small agentic workflow that orchestrates multiple tools and records a structured trace of what it used.

Standard Graph RAG
  • Embed the question into a vector.
  • Do a similarity search over documents.
  • Optionally expand via graph neighbors.
  • Concatenate context and send once to the LLM.
  • Limited visibility into which systems were used.
Johny 5 · Agentic Graph RAG
  • Step 1: Understand question and detect tickers (see understand_question in Trace & Steps).
  • Step 2: Call profiles DB for company metadata (retrieve_profiles / companies_lookup).
  • Step 3: Call external metrics API placeholder (fetch_external_metrics / external_metrics_api).
  • Step 4: Call news / filings source (retrieve_news / news_lookup), populated from Yahoo Finance RSS in this demo.
  • Step 5: Combine graph structure, metrics and news into a single synthesized answer, with a trace you can audit.

In production at work, each of these tools can point at your internal systems (portfolio exposure, risk models, internal ratings, proprietary news feeds). The key value is that the agent calls them explicitly and leaves a structured trail of what data it used to answer the question.
